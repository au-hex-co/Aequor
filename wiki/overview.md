# Overview

**Merchant of Fate** is a pirate roguelike shipbuilder and 2D naval combat game set on the world of Terra, where three dragon-gods — [[Tellus]], [[Aequor (deity)|Aequor]], and [[Anima]] — made the first man, [[Primum]], and paid for every gift they gave him.

The core theme, stated outright in the pitch and enforced mechanically through relics like [[Nectaris]], is that **a price is always exacted** — nothing is simply given, dodged, or survived, only deferred.

The player's own identity follows the same rule: [[The Protagonist]] is not fixed at the start, but earned and lost across a three-name arc (Cede Amare → Cedere → Caedere) that mirrors the game's philosophy of debt.

History is tracked via [[Timeline of the Aetas]], ages named retroactively for their defining event — including the current, still-unnamed era the player is writing.
