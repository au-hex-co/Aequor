# Primum

The first man, made by [[Tellus]], [[Aequor|Aequor]], and [[Anima]] out of boredom with their own creation. Given three gifts — agency, wisdom, and duty — each paid for in flesh, blood, or divinity.

His descendants scattered into clans, each shaped by a piece of the original bargain, including [[Ferus]], the firstborn made before agency was understood.
