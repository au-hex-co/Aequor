# Aequor (deity)

One of the three first beings, dragon of the ocean. Along with [[Tellus]] and [[Anima]], Aequor helped create [[Primum]], the first man. His domain is **History**, sourced from a formless, prior force older than the Three themselves — see [[River of Knowledge]]. His Ichor — his own magic, channeled into [[Silvia]] during her training (see [[Lore in order]], Ch. 3) — runs **teal**, the same shade [[Nexus]] is born with, unexplained; see [[Nexum]].

## The one who ends his brothers

Aequor is the one who carries out both endings among the Three. He is the one who, at [[Anima]]'s own request, thrusts his talon into him and tears out his heart so agency could pass beyond a single creation (see [[Lore in order]], Chapter 4) — an act of necessity, not malice, but Anima survives it: maimed and sealed away with his kingdom of clouds, not killed.

Centuries later, when the freed and half-mad [[Tellus]] rampages through the age of disaster following [[Nexus]]'s death (see [[Tellus]]), it is Aequor again who lands the final blow — this time for good. Binding one dragon, rather than killing him, takes two — and by the time Tellus breaks loose, Aequor is the only dragon left unsealed and uncorrupted to do either: [[Anima]] is already maimed and sealed away, and Tellus himself is the one who would need binding. With millions dying under Tellus's rampage and no second dragon anywhere in the world, Aequor doesn't choose the kill over the bind — there is no bind available to choose. Tellus does not survive it the way Anima did; there is no fusion left to shelter in. Aequor ends up, by his own hand, the one who has maimed one brother and killed the other — not out of malice either time, but because both times, he was the only one left who could act.

> [!key-insight] Naming
> The vault itself is named **Aequor** after this god — the ocean dragon presiding over a pirate/naval game about debts that outlive their gods. Worth leaning into thematically (title screen, splash text, etc.) if it isn't already intentional.

> [!key-insight] Gift resolved (2026-09-09)
> Aequor's gift to Primum is **wisdom** — stated outright in [[Lore in order]], Ch. 4 ("Aequor, brother of wisdom, filled Primum with wisdom and resolve"), not merely inferred as previously noted here. The domain underneath that gift is **History**: see [[River of Knowledge]] for the formless source force, growing-block time, and how History/Fate/Agency form an equally-weighted triangle across the Three.

> [!gap] Still undeveloped
> Aequor has no confirmed relic or era of worship in the source pitch (unlike [[Tellus]] → [[Nectaris]] and Terra/Manus eras, or [[Anima]] → Aetas of Gods). Given the game is naval/ocean-based, Aequor may deserve more direct presence — a second relic, a patron era, or a role in the ending.
