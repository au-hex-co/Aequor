# Corvinus

A distant descendant of [[Nexus]] — by the time of Nexus's death, the Fae King's own children have long since died out, and Corvinus is kin by a much older, more distant line. Pale, with skin that takes on a teal hue under strong emotion.

## Fate: The Second Fae King

Present at the ruined castle when Nexus finally dies (see [[Nexus]]), Corvinus is called forward by the dying king — "Little raven" — and ordered to swear a formal oath: to lead the Fae to a brighter future, to be a better king than Nexus was. He doesn't believe he can be — how does anyone outdo "the immortal king of honor"? — and says so mid-oath. It doesn't matter. Nexum holds him to the words regardless of what he believes: the moment the oath is complete, Nexus's spilled Ichor rises into golden chains that physically compel Corvinus to leave his dying king and go rule, whether he feels ready or not (see [[Nexum]] — active/formal contracts). The chains solidify into a crown as he goes, marking him the second Fae King.
