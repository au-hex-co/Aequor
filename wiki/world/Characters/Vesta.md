## [[overview|Overview]]

[[Vesta]] is the second human, and the first woman, shaped upon Terra. Where [[Primum]] was formed whole from the dust of [[Tellus]], [[Vesta]] was formed from [[Primum]] himself — his torn flesh reshaped by [[Tellus]], bound with water by [[Aequor]], and given breath and agency by [[Anima]]. She did not enter the world as a separate creation but as a piece of another made new. Her existence is a price paid, and by the end of her story she becomes something far greater than the companion she was made to be: the Maiden of the Fog, and later, Mother of the Forest.

## Origin: Flesh Made New

[[Vesta]] exists because [[Primum]] could not answer a simple question — if his purpose was to entertain the gods, who was left to entertain _him_? The Three had no answer, only a warning: a price is always exacted for what fate bestows.

[[Primum]] accepted that price without knowing its shape. [[Tellus]] tore the flesh from [[Primum]]'s left arm and the bone from his right leg, and from that same flesh he shaped a new form. [[Aequor]] poured water through it until it held together, and [[Anima]] bent low and breathed into it the same breath once given to [[Primum]]. When she opened her eyes, the Three named her [[Vesta]].

She was not made from the earth like [[Primum]]. She was made from _him_ — his agony became her flesh, his wholeness the price paid for her.

## Life With Primum

For twenty years, [[Vesta]] and [[Primum]] lived in quiet balance. [[Primum]] left each day to fulfill his purpose, entertaining and keeping company with the Three, while [[Vesta]] kept the home he had built. It was a plain, domestic peace — until [[Primum]]'s mortality caught up with him, and with it, a desire neither of them had names for yet: to become parents, to leave something behind on the planet they called home.

## Chosen by Tellus

When the Three considered granting fertility, it was [[Vesta]] that [[Tellus]] turned to look at directly — not [[Primum]]. She was the one who stammered a frightened "yes" beneath the gaze of a god's massive eye. It was [[Vesta]], not [[Primum]], who would carry every price this gift demanded.

[[Tellus]] handed [[Primum]] the apple, but it was [[Vesta]]'s body that would pay for what it granted.

## The First Birth: Ferus

Nine days after eating the apple, [[Vesta]] was alone when the pain came. [[Primum]] was away, off entertaining the gods as always, and [[Vesta]] crawled toward the door of their home in agony, her flesh tearing as she went. She collapsed outside — and it was not [[Primum]] who came to her aid, but the forest itself. Vines crawled to her, trees leaned to shield her from the sun, and when she woke the next morning in her bed, whole and unmarked, the trees outside seemed to shiver in greeting, as though they already recognized her as something more than human.

The child born from that agony was [[Ferus]] — loved, but lacking speech, lacking the free will that had been given to [[Primum]]. He moved on instinct alone.

## The Price for Agency

It was [[Primum]] who begged the gods to grant his children agency, but it was [[Vesta]] who was handed the cost. [[Tellus]] and [[Aequor]] came to her — not to [[Primum]] — carrying a smooth golden orb they called _[[Sacrarium]]_, telling her that eating it would let her children breathe of their own volition. [[Vesta]] hesitated, wary of the pain fertility had already cost her, but she could not refuse a god, and after days of [[Primum]]'s urging she finally ate it.

What followed was stranger and crueler than before. For ten days [[Vesta]] felt only hunger, not pregnancy. Then, two months later, she felt something alive inside her. Nine months after that — thirty times the ordinary span, and thirty times the ordinary pain — her second labor began.

## Mother of the Forest

[[Primum]] was, once again, away with the gods when [[Vesta]]'s second child came. Alone, in unbearable pain, her abdomen tearing open, she screamed into the forest for help that no human answered. But the trees heard her. Roots tore through the roof of her home to hold her steady, and one root reached into her mind itself.

In her agony, [[Vesta]] unknowingly gave the nearest tree the gift she didn't know she carried — the ability to speak, to think. The forest spoke back to her for the first time, naming what she had become:

> "Oh Maiden of the fog, rest, for when you wake, the pain you feel we will remember in your place. Oh Mother of the forest, rest, for now you create life."

[[Vesta]] did not set out to become divine. She was shaped from another's sacrifice, chosen by a god without her consent, and made to bear the full physical cost of gifts that were asked for on her behalf. Yet by her second act of childbirth, she had bound herself to the forest itself, transforming from a companion fashioned to end [[Primum]]'s loneliness into a mother whose pain the trees now carry for her.
