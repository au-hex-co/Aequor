# Levinus

An elf, and [[Silvia]]'s mate — arriving in her life after her one, single mating with [[Ferus]], her elder sibling born without agency.

## Gifts: wisdom and duty

Where Ferus was made on instinct alone, before agency was understood — no wisdom, no duty, no choice in what he was — Levinus is given both, deliberately:

- **Wisdom, from [[Aequor]].** The same dragon who has spent years pouring History's Ichor into Silvia ("let my Ichor flood you") extends that teaching to the man who would stand beside her.
- **Duty, from [[Tellus]].** The god of Fate gifts Levinus duty directly, the way he once gifted it to [[Primum]] — a personal act, not the universal Nexum law, which does not yet exist in Silvia's lifetime (see [[Nexum]]).

Together the two gifts make Levinus the deliberate answer to what Ferus lacked: a mate who can reason, and a mate who can be held to his word.

## Notes

- Arrives after the Ferus encounter, not before — framed as what Silvia turns to once that chapter of her life is behind her.
- First named elf in the lore; whether elves are an established people elsewhere or Levinus is singular to this story is undeveloped.
