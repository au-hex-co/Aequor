# Anima

One of the three first beings, dragon of the wind. Along with [[Tellus]] and [[Aequor|Aequor]], Anima helped create [[Primum]], the first man, and gave him the gift of **agency**.

Anima tore out his own heart so his children's children could breathe free — agency as breath, paid for in his own flesh. [[Aequor]] is the one who delivers the cut, at Anima's own request. His torn-out heart, sealed away with his kingdom of clouds, resurfaces as the golden orb [[Tellus]] and [[Aequor]] carry to [[Vesta]] and name **[[Sacrarium]]** — the mechanism by which agency now passes down her line instead of being granted to each child individually.

**Anima is maimed, not killed.** The text never states his death outright — only that the golden light in his eye faded, that he went rabid from the pain, and that his kingdom of clouds sealed itself away. Read against [[Tellus]]'s later fate (who *is* finally, fully killed by Aequor at the end of the Malum, see [[Tellus]] and [[Nexus]]), Anima's ending is deliberately the lesser one: broken, heartless, sealed away and presumably still mad within his own kingdom, but not ended the way Tellus eventually is. Aequor is the hand behind both endings — see [[Aequor]].

**Aetas of Gods** (1310–1002 BM): worship centers on the brothers, chiefly Anima.
