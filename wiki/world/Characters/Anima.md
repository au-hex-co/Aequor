# Anima

One of the three first beings, dragon of the wind. Along with [[Tellus]] and [[Aequor|Aequor]], Anima helped create [[Primum]], the first man, and gave him the gift of **agency**.

Anima tore out his own heart so his children's children could breathe free — the wind god's death is tied directly to the ability to breathe (agency/free will as breath). His torn-out heart, sealed away with his kingdom of clouds, resurfaces as the golden orb [[Tellus]] and [[Aequor]] carry to [[Vesta]] and name **[[Sacrarium]]** — the mechanism by which agency now passes down her line instead of being granted to each child individually.

**Aetas of Gods** (1310–1002 BM): worship centers on the brothers, chiefly Anima.
