# Nectaris

Nectaris is the heart of [[Tellus]], dragon of fate, order, and earth — a relic said to hold the last of his own blood, spilled when humanity betrayed him at the Malum (see [[Timeline of the Aetas]]).

The player may interact with it early on without knowing what it is until late game. It is intended to be the strongest artifact in the game, and the design's proof of concept for how myth becomes mechanic (see [[Relics]]).

## The Deferred Fate System

Nectaris cannot cancel fate, only delay where and when it lands. Anything dodged, redirected, or rewound does not disappear — it queues. Queued fate comes due automatically, without warning, in a future encounter, landing at the worst possible moment. The relic does not protect you. It negotiates a deadline extension, and negotiates badly.

## Lesser Tier (passive / always active)

- **The Debt Remembers** — every wound this ship has ever taken is not forgotten. Damage previously suffered can be called due, inflicted retroactively on whoever strikes you next. Your scars become a ledger you collect on.
- **What the Ground Refuses** — for a duration, this stretch of sea belongs to Tellus and refuses to let anything sink here. Ships cannot be destroyed while inside it, only driven out. Death becomes banishment, not defeat.
- **Inheritance of the First Wound** — recalls [[Primum]]'s arm and leg torn away to make **[[Vesta]]**. Anything sacrificed — hull, crew, or cargo — is never simply lost. It is transmuted into something else of equal cost.

## Greater Tier (active, long cooldown, once per encounter)

- **The Earth Does Not Forgive** — echoes Manus's betrayal and Tellus's death. Once triggered, every point of damage you deal is treated as a debt the enemy incurred. Debts compound, escalating the longer the enemy survives.
- **Sortis, Unbroken** — you see the hit coming. You may delay it. You cannot cancel it. Every turn you delay, it grows — fate deferred compounds like debt. If never paid off before the fight ends, it rolls forward into your next encounter as a guaranteed opening strike.
- **The Mountain Was Once Flesh** — Tellus's mountains were the by-product of 108 years of divine boredom; the land was never natural, only discarded godhood. Your ship becomes terrain itself, unkillable but unable to act, then erupts back into the fight with everything it absorbed.
- **The Bleeding Earth** — Nectaris opens a wound in the sea floor itself. For a duration, all damage you or a chosen ally would take is instead drawn down into the earth. Nothing is healed, nothing is erased — the wound is only moved, and the earth remembers every hit it swallows, feeding *The Debt Remembers* when this ends.

## Divine Tier (once per run — Tellus acting through the relic, not the player)

- **Betrayal Given Shape** — the Malum happens again, briefly, in miniature. Every enemy on the field takes the damage Tellus took when Manus turned on him, scaled to how much damage you've taken this fight. Your suffering becomes the cataclysm.
- **I Am the Ground You Stand On** — for one turn, no enemy can act unless they act against you specifically. Tellus's gift of duty to Primum, inverted and weaponized, forcing the entire battlefield's obligation onto a single point.
- **The Nectar Does Not Run Dry** — Tellus's death should have ended his gift. The relic argues otherwise. Fully drain the resource pool once, and instead of ending, the ability resets to full and repeats, unprompted, later in the fight. The god refuses to actually be dead yet.
- **Fate's Reboot** — the ship's state (hull, crew, position) can be rewound to how it stood at the start of this encounter, as if the fight never touched you. Nothing is undone freely: every point of damage erased is added to a debt that comes due at the start of your very next encounter, delivered all at once, with no chance to defend against it. You did not survive this fight. You postponed it.

> [!key-insight] Design note (from source)
> *Sortis, Unbroken* and *Fate's Reboot* draw from the same Deferred Fate queue at different scales — one hit versus one encounter — and should be mutually exclusive as capstone picks, so a player cannot stack two deferral sources into a single future reckoning.

> [!gap] Undefined term: Vesta
> *Inheritance of the First Wound* references "Vesta" as what [[Primum]]'s severed arm and leg became. Not defined anywhere else in the source material — see [[Primum]].
