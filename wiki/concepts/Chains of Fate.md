# Chains of Fate

**Type:** In-universe concept — not physical, not a place or object; the force behind [[Tellus]]'s domain, the way [[River of Knowledge]] is the force behind [[Aequor]]'s.
**Origin:** Predates and exceeds the Three — the wellspring Tellus's domain draws from, though unlike History, Tellus himself *is* the source Fate was drawn from (see [[Tellus]])
**Domain:** Fate — what is already written, and cannot be otherwise
**Also known as:** Sors Fortuna ("fate's lot" — Latin, sors being a lot cast or drawn, never chosen — a name for the force itself)

## Description

It has no single shape either — it has been felt as a wheel, a scale, and chains, and none of these are quite right, because it is not any one of them. They are borrowed metaphors, not a true shape:

*A wheel, turning and turning, never arriving anywhere new. A scale, already tipped, the weight settled before it was asked. Chains, plain and cold, closed around a wrist that was never asked either.*

Whatever metaphor is active, it names a limit already in force, never a limit still being decided. That is the point, not a flaw in the description — [[Tellus]] is not merely subject to this force, he *is* the source it was drawn from, the way [[Aequor]] is only History's channel rather than its source (see [[River of Knowledge]]).

## Closed-script time

Where the River of Knowledge holds growing-block time, this force holds its direct antithesis:

- The future is not open. It is already written — not hidden, not undecided, simply not yet arrived at.
- "The present" is only the place the already-finished script happens to be read aloud, not a live edge where anything new is added.
- Nothing is "yet to discover." There is nothing left outside the script to discover.

See [[River of Knowledge]]#Growing-block time for the fuller three-way split, and [[Cosmology]] for how this plays out for [[Tellus]] specifically.

## The three images

- **Wheel** — a fixed, cyclical path; motion that only ever returns to where it started, never a genuinely open road.
- **Scale** — a verdict already weighed and settled, not still being decided.
- **Chains** — the literal restraint on the human body; the physical, bodily form Fate's closure takes, as opposed to History's abstract openness.

Like the [[River of Knowledge]]'s `~` marker, these are meant to recur through pattern and image rather than be named on the page as "symbols of Fate" — the reader should come to feel the wheel/scale/chains as Tellus's and fate's through repetition, not exposition.

## Notes
