# Core Gameplay

Three pillars:

- **Roguelike run structure** — build and upgrade a ship between encounters; permadeath-adjacent stakes; choices compound across a run.
- **2D naval combat** — tactical, resource-driven, built around the same "price is always exacted" logic as the mythology (see [[Cosmology]]).
- **Relics as mythology made mechanical** — rare items don't just grant stats, they enact a piece of the world's own history when equipped. See [[Relics]] and the flagship example, [[Nectaris]].

## Tone & Influences

Myth-driven worldbuilding in the register of classical scripture and oral tradition; mechanical systems that make abstract cost and consequence tangible and playable; a protagonist arc built on the idea that names, like debts, are earned rather than given (see [[The Protagonist]]).
