#### Chapter 9: A God's Quarrel

As the golden light settled, there was one final aftershock. A massive pulse of gold burst from the Aurora.

Aurora then fell to the floor and started crying, the cry was ragged and sounded foreign.

**SLAM**

The door was pushed open by three elvish males who stood shocked watching Silvia hold the frail girl while she was slowly calming down. 

As the men stood speechless, Serena pushed past and showed a similar sign of shock at the wailing coming from Aurora.

Lev who heard the commotion and saw the massive golden flash also ran full speed and barged through the people and saw...

Silvia was on the floor, with the head of Aurora in her lap. As she looked at the elves they saw flecks of gold overwhelm her eyes, making them look more gold then silver. 

Finally breaking the silence one of the men yelled out, "The girl has been blessed by Anima, She is able to give life to the forsaken."

This yell was met with a massive cheer as infectious as ever as it spread across the village.

Serena walked up cautiously and asked, "How did you do it girl?"

At first, Silvia thought Serena looked mad before she saw, Serena's eyes were filled with tears.
And as Silvia watched she saw Serena approach Aurora and gently caress her. 

The second Serena touched the young girl, the girl flinched before leaning into the hand and nuzzling it. "What's her name?" Serena asked.

"What do you mean?" Silvia stammered still shaken.

"Lord Anima always named those he breathed life in.. So what do you name her?"

Silvia finally spoke, "Aurora."

Following Serena's lead the elves took Aurora and took her to get food and get cleaned up, leaving only Lev and Silvia behind. 

"So that's why" Lev said. "That's why you care for the forsaken, because you can help them."

Though he said it neutrally he felt conflicted 

Silvia was quiet before finally saying, "I saw Anima."

Lev instantly was dumbfounded before rushing Silvia and whispering, "Tell me more."

Though Silvia was stunned by the events before her she retold from seeing the city gates and finally meeting the chained god. 

Upon hearing Anima's condition Lev was horrified.

"You have to save him!" Lev said as he grabbed Silvia's hands, "You have to!"

---

Silvia went along the river and followed the creek, on her way, people looked for a second before looking right back at the miracle girl who was crying while being hoisted above the village.

Silvia walked out to no resistance, and reached a fimiliar spot and called out, "Aequor!"

As she waited she felt the sorrunding forest go quiet and the air thickening. 
"You seem agitated" A gentle voice replied from behind Silvia.

"I found Anima. He has been trapped there how can I help free him?" Silvia said jumping to face Aequor. 

The bank seemed to darken as clouds blotted out the sky, "You cannot free him"

Silvia oblivious to the clouds continued, "Why not? There has to be a way."

Aequor just stared at Silvia and said, "Those chains are near unbreakable the only way to help Anima would be to sacrifice a girl with sacarium, and there are only two. You and your mother."

Silvia at those words seemed dejected, "I have sacrarium, Can I give him some? I don't want anyone to die."

"Silvia" The giant dragon said gently as it rested its head on the bank. "You need to understand, under no circumstance can you give ANY of your sacrarium to him. He is in a very bad state..."

"How do you know what state he is in?" Silvia interupted. 
"You helped didn't you, You hurt Anima? Why?" Silvia coutinued scared 

Aequor just gently held her gaze and continued, "You don't understand and that's fine." 

"NO!" Silvia shouted back as a tear rolled down her cheek, "I thought you were kind, I thought you wouldn't hurt anyone."

"Silvia," Aequor said before flying into the air and going away.

"What did Lord Aequor say?"

Silvia heard a voice come from behind her and she turned around to see Lev.

"Oh nothing, I asked him to help me free Anima, he said we need to kill someone with sacrarium, I said no. why?" 

Lev seemed to stiffen before asking, "What is Sacrarium"

Silvia was sort of confused before saying, "I see it like a golden shard like inside my belly."

---

As evening came, all the elves took Aurora to a celebration leaving the rest of the forsaken with Silvia and Lev.

As Silvia was ready to go talk to Anima she softly asked "Eluvia?" 

"Yes miss" the friendly voice answered back. 

"Can I please have three more petals?" Silvia asked.

Shortly after which the spirit returned with three more petals.
Silvia looked at the three irresestable petals with the shimmery skin and swallowed them.

Slowly Silvia drifted off to sleep before waking up in a dark damp watery plane, The water barely was more than a finger deep, but there was no light and nothing else in the abyss.

She turned in circles before yelling out, "Hello?"

"Why should I bother when you can just rip out of my thrall?" A young boyish voice asked.

"I have had you in my grasp so close to fruition just for you to have ichor... The world really is a sick joke!" 

Silvia turned around to see... It was a young boy in white with a weird looking fabric covering his whole body. His hair was white with black tips and he stood on top of the water not in it.

"What do you mean? Who are you?" Silvia asked back. 

"I have no name nor have I been given a name but feel free to do so..." he replied back.

"But I don't know you." Silvia replied 

The boy grinned and replied, "Yes you do!"

As he spoke the water arround them rippiled before tranforming into a wave turning the gray plane underneath into a green and grassy feild, the sky gradually became blue as the trees sprouted out of the ground before growing near instally, Suddenly before silvias eyes, she was in her clearning again, and Infront of her the boy.

Silvia said "I'm In a dream, You are the stellaberry!"

The second she said that she saw the colors in the trees and grass fade to black, the only thing in the gray landscape with color was the boy who was glowing white with now red eyes.

"I guess stella could work" The boy said as he transformed into a girl, giving way to his arms turning into vines.

Silvia panicking she reached inside and tried to grab the shard of sacrarium, but it was too small, the rest of her shard was inside Aurora. 

The Stellaberry apporached her slowly before asking to her face. "You wouldn't leave me now right. I'm so Lonely, So hungry..."

Just as she felt the vines touch her she felt herself snap back to her body and when she opened her eyes she saw a golden castle infront of her...

Silvia travelled through the foggy city before taking the leaf up, finally when she awoke on the top floor she turned and saw the chained dragon slowly open its eyes. 

"You returned!" He said weakly.
