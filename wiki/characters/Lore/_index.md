# Lore

- [[Lore in order]] — the canonical, chronological telling of the origin myth (creation of Primum, the making of Vesta, the births of Ferus and Silvia, the founding of the Sacrarium). This is the single source of truth for these scenes — earlier draft fragments have been retired now that this exists.
