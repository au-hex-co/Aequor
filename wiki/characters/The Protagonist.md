# The Protagonist

The player character's identity is not fixed at the start; it is earned, and lost, across three names:

1. **Cede Amare** — who they begin as: someone who yields to love, to duty, to the world's terms.
2. **Cedere** — who they become mid-journey: someone learning to yield only on their own terms, to give ground deliberately rather than out of weakness.
3. **Caedere** — who they end as: to cut. To stop yielding entirely, and start taking payment instead.

> [!key-insight] The name arc is not cosmetic
> It mirrors the game's core philosophy: every gift, every mercy, every dodge has a price, and the question is only ever who ends up paying it. See [[Core Gameplay]] and [[Nectaris]] for how this is enforced mechanically, not just narratively.
