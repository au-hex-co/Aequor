# The Protagonist

The player character's identity is not fixed at the start; it is earned, and lost, across three names. See [[Cedere]] for the in-game character card.

## The Name Arc

| Name | Who they are | When |
|---|---|---|
| **Cede Amare** | Someone who yields — to love, to duty, to the world's terms | Who they begin as |
| **Cedere** | Someone learning to yield only on their own terms, to give ground deliberately rather than out of weakness | Who they become mid-journey |
| **Caedere** | To cut. To stop yielding entirely, and start taking payment instead | Who they end as |

> [!key-insight] The arc is not cosmetic
> It mirrors the game's core philosophy — every gift, every mercy, every dodge has a price, and the question is only ever who ends up paying it. See [[Cosmology]] and [[Relics]] for the same rule expressed mythologically and mechanically.

> [!gap] Undeveloped
> The source pitch states the three names and their meaning but not the triggers that move the player between them — whether it's tied to run count, specific choices, or story beats. Needs a design decision before it can drive UI or narrative pacing.
