## Key Recent Facts
- **Merchant of Fate**: pirate roguelike, ship-building + 2D naval combat, myth-driven ("a price is always exacted").
- Three gods (Tellus/earth, Aequor/ocean, Anima/wind) made the first man, [[Primum]], and paid for it. Tellus died in the Malum (betrayed by humanity/"Manus"), splitting the calendar into BM/AM.
- Protagonist's identity is earned across three names: Cede Amare → Cedere → Caedere.
- Flagship relic [[Nectaris]] (Tellus's heart) mechanically enforces the "nothing is free, only deferred" theme via a 3-tier ability list.
