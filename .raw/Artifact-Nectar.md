# Nectaris

Nectaris is the heart of the dragon of fate Telus, it controls order fate and earth, it a artifact that the player might interact with early on but not know what it is till late game, this will be the strongest artifact in the game

NECTARIS — ABILITY LIST

THE DEFERRED FATE SYSTEM
Nectaris cannot cancel fate, only delay where and when it lands. Anything dodged, redirected, or rewound does not disappear, it queues. Queued fate comes due automatically, without warning, in a future encounter, landing at the worst possible moment. The relic does not protect you. It negotiates a deadline extension, and negotiates badly.

LESSER TIER (passive / always active)

The Debt Remembers
Every wound this ship has ever taken is not forgotten. Damage previously suffered can be called due, inflicted retroactively on whoever strikes you next. Your scars become a ledger you collect on.

What the Ground Refuses
For a duration, this stretch of sea belongs to Tellus and refuses to let anything sink here. Ships cannot be destroyed while inside it, only driven out. Death becomes banishment, not defeat.

Inheritance of the First Wound
Recalls Primum's arm and leg torn away to make Vesta. Anything sacrificed, hull, crew, or cargo, is never simply lost. It is transmuted into something else of equal cost.

GREATER TIER (active, long cooldown, once per encounter)

The Earth Does Not Forgive
Echoes Manus's betrayal and Tellus's death. Once triggered, every point of damage you deal is treated as a debt the enemy incurred. Debts compound, escalating the longer the enemy survives.

Sortis, Unbroken
You see the hit coming. You may delay it. You cannot cancel it. Every turn you delay, it grows, fate deferred compounds like debt. If never paid off before the fight ends, it does not vanish, it rolls forward into your next encounter as a guaranteed opening strike.

The Mountain Was Once Flesh
Tellus's mountains were the by-product of 108 years of divine boredom. The land was never natural, only discarded godhood. Your ship becomes terrain itself, unkillable but unable to act, then erupts back into the fight with everything it absorbed.

The Bleeding Earth
Nectaris opens a wound in the sea floor itself. For a duration, all damage you or a chosen ally would take is instead drawn down into the earth. Nothing is healed, nothing is erased, the wound is only moved, and the earth remembers every hit it swallows, feeding The Debt Remembers when this ends.

DIVINE TIER (once per run, Tellus acting through the relic, not the player)

Betrayal Given Shape
The Malum happens again, briefly, in miniature. Every enemy on the field takes the damage Tellus took when Manus turned on him, scaled to how much damage you've taken this fight. Your suffering becomes the cataclysm.

I Am the Ground You Stand On
For one turn, no enemy can act unless they act against you specifically. Tellus's gift of duty to Primum, inverted and weaponized, forcing the entire battlefield's obligation onto a single point.

The Nectar Does Not Run Dry
Tellus's death should have ended his gift. The relic argues otherwise. Fully drain the resource pool once, and instead of ending, the ability resets to full and repeats, unprompted, later in the fight. The god refuses to actually be dead yet.

Fate's Reboot
Tellus once told Primum, a price is always exacted for what fate bestows. Nectaris does not forget this even when the player wishes it would. The ship's state, hull, crew, and position, can be rewound to how it stood at the start of this encounter, as if the fight never touched you. Nothing is undone freely: every point of damage erased is added to a debt that comes due at the start of your very next encounter, delivered all at once, with no chance to defend against it. You did not survive this fight. You postponed it.

DESIGN NOTE
Sortis, Unbroken and Fate's Reboot draw from the same Deferred Fate queue at different scales, one hit versus one encounter, and should be mutually exclusive as capstone picks so a player cannot stack two deferral sources into a single future reckoning.
