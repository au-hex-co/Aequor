Timeline of the Aetas

History is measured in Aetas — ages named for the event that defined them, not assigned in advance. The current age remains unnamed for exactly that reason: it hasn't happened yet.

Dates are given as BM (Before Malum) / AM (After Malum)

  1. 3001–2793 BM · 209 yrs

    Aetas of Creation

    The brothers create Man (Primum)

  2. 2792–2435 BM · 358 yrs

    Aetas of Man

    The children of Primum form clans

  3. 2434–2301 BM · 134 yrs

    Aetas of Peace

    Humans build cities and trade for goods

  4. 2300–1311 BM · 990 yrs

    Aetas of War

    A thousand-year war between clans; Ferrin nearly wiped out

  5. 1310–1002 BM · 309 yrs

    Aetas of Gods

    Worship centers on the brothers, chiefly Anima

  6. 1001–775 BM · 227 yrs

    Aetas of Atlan

    Age of exploration; the Atlan clan maps the world

  7. 774–542 BM · 233 yrs

    Aetas of Terra

    Worship shifts to Tellus, dragon of the earth

  8. 541–0 BM · 542 yrs

    Aetas of Manus

    Humanity betrays the gods, leading to the death of Tellus

  9. Year 0 · —

    The Malum

    The death of Tellus

  10. 1 AM onward · ongoing

    Current Era

    Unnamed — awaiting its defining event

Key InsightFerrin vs. Ferus

This table names the clan "Ferrin" as nearly wiped out in the Aetas of War. The main pitch text names the firstborn "Ferus." See The clans and Ferus — treated as the same lineage (Ferrin = "the line of Ferus"), pending confirmation with the author.

Key InsightThe current era is a design hook

The unnamed "Current Era" is explicitly where the player's run(s) take place, and where Cedere's name arc plays out. Whatever the player does may retroactively become the defining event that names this Aetas — a strong narrative frame for run-to-run stakes.
