Cosmology

worlddevelopingupdated 2026-09-01#world#cosmology

Three beings — Tellus of the earth, Aequor of the ocean, and Anima of the wind — were the first things to wake on the world of Terra. Bored of their own creation (mountains, trenches, a hundred years of increasingly strange animals), they turned to making a man: Primum, shaped from mud, water, and breath in equal parts. See Lore in order for the full telling.

Each god paid a gift into Primum in a currency that was not his to spend:

GodGift to PrimumPrice paid
TellusDutyLater betrayed by the humanity he gave it to — see the Malum, below
AequorWisdom (inferred — see Aequor)Not stated in the source pitch
AnimaAgencyHis own heart, torn out so agency could pass beyond a single creation

Primum's descendants scattered into clans, each shaped by a piece of that original bargain — see The clans. Ferus, the firstborn, was made on instinct alone before agency was understood, and was never counted among the true children — a wound the family carries into its own naming conventions generations later.

The gods did not survive their own myth

  • Anima tore out his own heart so his children's children could breathe free of their own volition (see Sacrarium).
  • Tellus was betrayed by the very humanity he gave duty to — an event so total it splits the entire calendar in two: Before Malum (BM) and After Malum (AM). See Timeline of the Aetas.
  • Aequor alone has no confirmed death or fate in the source material — see the gap noted on his page.

The current era has no name yet. It is still being written, by whoever the player becomes — see The Protagonist.

Key InsightThe rule beneath the myth

Every gift in this cosmology is paid for in flesh, blood, or divinity. Relics and Nectaris turn that same rule into a combat mechanic: no relic offers a free escape, only a deferred price.
