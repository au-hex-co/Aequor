Aequor (deity)

worldstubupdated 2026-08-24#world#deity

One of the three first beings, dragon of the ocean. Along with Tellus and Anima, Aequor helped create Primum, the first man.

Key InsightNaming

The vault itself is named Aequor after this god — the ocean dragon presiding over a pirate/naval game about debts that outlive their gods. Worth leaning into thematically (title screen, splash text, etc.) if it isn't already intentional.

GapUndeveloped

Aequor has no confirmed gift, relic, or era of worship in the source pitch (unlike Tellus → Nectaris and Terra/Manus eras, or Anima → Aetas of Gods). By elimination Aequor likely gave wisdom (see Cosmology), but this is inferred, not stated. Given the game is naval/ocean-based, Aequor may deserve more direct presence — a second relic, a patron era, or a role in the ending.
