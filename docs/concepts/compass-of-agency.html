    Concepts/Compass of Agency

    Compass of Agency

    conceptdevelopingupdated 2026-09-11#concept#aequor#cosmology#agency

    Type: In-universe concept — not physical, not a place or object; the force behind Anima's domain, the way River of Knowledge is the force behind Aequor's and Chains of Fate is the force behind Tellus's. Origin: Predates and exceeds the Three — the wellspring Anima's domain draws from, rather than Anima himself (he channels it, and paid his own heart to loose it outward, the way Aequor channels the River rather than being it — unlike Tellus, who is the source Fate was drawn from; see Tellus) Domain: Agency — the self's power to choose, not yet settled into either a record or a script Also known as: Liberum Arbitrium ("free judgment" — Latin, the philosophers' own name for free will — a name for the force itself)

    Description

    It has no shape either — it has been felt as a compass, a key, and a quill, and none of these are quite right, because it is not any one of them. They are borrowed metaphors, not a true shape:

    A compass, needle trembling toward a direction not yet walked. A key, turned in a lock that was never supposed to open. A quill, lifted an instant before it touches the page — the choice made, but not yet written down.

    Whatever metaphor is active, it names a choice still being made, never one already recorded or already closed. That is the point, not a flaw in the description — Anima is not merely a channel for this force the way Aequor channels River of Knowledge, he is the one who paid to make it reach beyond himself at all: his own heart, torn out so it could pass to others as Sacrarium.

    The choosing instant

    Where the River of Knowledge holds growing-block time, and Chains of Fate holds its closed-script antithesis, this force holds neither — it is the hinge between them, not a third timeline:

    • It cannot exist against a closed script. If the future is already written, there is nothing left to choose (see Chains of Fate).
    • It cannot exist without a record behind it either. A self has to be built out of something to do the choosing, and that something is History (see River of Knowledge).
    • The instant of choosing is never itself the record. The moment the choice is made, it stops being agency and starts being History — a page the River writes next, or a link the Chains foreclose.

    See Cosmology for the fuller three-way split between History, Fate, and Agency.

    The three images

    • Compass — the title image, the way chains is Chains of Fate's and river is River of Knowledge's: unlike a key, which opens one door already standing locked, a compass only ever points toward a direction not yet walked — the live act of choosing, before it hardens into a record or is foreclosed by a script.
    • Key — the literal object of unlocking; agency as the freedom to enter what was otherwise barred.
    • Quill — the instant before a choice is set down; the hinge point where agency ends and History's page begins.

    Like the River of Knowledge's ~ marker and Chains of Fate's wheel/scale/chains, these are meant to recur through pattern and image rather than be named on the page as "symbols of Agency" — the reader should come to feel the compass/key/quill as Anima's through repetition, not exposition.

    Notes

    • The Narrator finds this force literally nestled inside the prologue's chained book: a gold feather, a quill not yet lifted, waiting inside History's pages under Fate's own chains.
