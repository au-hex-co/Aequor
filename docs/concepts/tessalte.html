    Concepts/Tessalte

    Tessalte

    conceptdevelopingupdated 2026-09-11#concept#cosmology#dimension#agency

    Type: A dimension — real, occupied geometry, not a platonic concept like River of Knowledge Structure: 3 axes — Want, Cost, Direction, nested rather than independent Sibling dimensions: 3 physical dimensions · 1 temporal dimension (generated by River of Knowledge, not a peer dimension itself — see below) Linked domain: Agency — but Tessalte is the medium Agency's own domain-force writes onto, not the domain itself (see below)

    Not Anima's domain

    Tessalte is a dimension the same way length, width, and height are — real, geometric, something a being actually occupies a shape within. River of Knowledge is the opposite kind of thing: platonic, shapeless, no location or body of its own, only information. Agency's true domain is a force of that second, platonic kind — something that predates and exceeds Anima, the way the River predates and exceeds Aequor — and it is not yet named. Tessalte is only the stage that force writes onto, the way physical space is the stage Tellus's own (also unnamed) domain-force writes onto.

    GapOpen — the two missing domain-objects

    Aequor is the only one of the Three whose platonic domain-object has a name and a page (River of Knowledge). Tellus and Anima only have their mortal-facing mechanics (Nexum, Sacrarium) — which River of Knowledge.md already treats as a different category of thing entirely ("no game mechanic attached, unlike Nectaris, Sacrarium, Nexum"). Candidate directions discussed but not settled: - Tellus's domain-object — a finished, already-written book, mirroring the River's "book still being written" (Cosmology already calls Fate "a closed script rather than a still-growing record" — this would just be naming that book). - Anima's domain-object — a wind/breath image, tying to Anima as dragon of the wind and "agency as breath" (Anima). Until these are named, Tessalte and the physical dimensions are stages without a credited author.

    Structure: Want → Cost → Direction

    The 3 Tessalte axes are not three independent sliders. They nest the way physical dimensions do — a line swept along a second axis makes a plane, a plane swept along a third makes a volume:

    AxisAnalogWhat it is
    Wanta linea single scalar pull toward or away from something — the atomic unit
    Costa planeWant swept along a second axis — many Wants held and weighed against each other at once
    Directiona volumeCost swept along a third axis — the resolved, acted-on choice; the one axis that's actually occupied

    Moving along Direction is choosing. Moving along Cost is re-weighing what you'd trade for what. Moving along Want is the desire shifting underneath both.

    A being's will as a 4D shape

    At any instant, a being's will is a 3D solid in Tessalte space — an extent along Want, Cost, and Direction, not a single point. Dragged forward by time, that solid traces a 4D history the way a physical object traces a worldline through spacetime: the full shape of everything a being wanted, weighed, and chose, laid out across a life.

    Sacrarium as access and magnitude

    Sacrarium's quantity is not a separate fuel source that happens to unlock Tessalte movement — it is the measurement of how much Tessalte volume a being can sustain. Access and strength are the same fact read two ways. This folds Sacrarium's existing mechanics in directly:

    • Depletion (through childbearing, theft, or never inheriting enough) shrinks the volume of will a being has room to occupy.
    • A being with none has zero Tessalte volume — no shape of their own to steer. Ferus, made on instinct alone before Sacrarium existed to carry, reads as exactly this: agency exercised on him from outside, case by case, because there was no dimensionality of his own to move through.

    Relationship to time

    Time is not a dimension standing alongside the physical and Tessalte ones as a seventh peer — it's the process that writes both of them. River of Knowledge is that process, and it runs on A-theory, not B-theory: not a passive coordinate laid out for reality to sit along, but an axis of reality — the active edge where the future is written because it does not yet exist (see River of Knowledge.md's growing-block time).

    Once a page is written, it settles into a fixed record — and from inside that record, both physical and Tessalte content look like they sit along an ordinary passive time-axis, block-universe style. That appearance is downstream of the River's writing, not the fundamental nature of time itself.

    Because the River is the generative process and Tessalte is only ever the thing being generated, the River can reach into Tessalte directly and overwrite it — author a being's will rather than merely record what it chose. This is not a new power grafted on; it's the same authority pattern already running through the myth, where Aequor is consistently the one who acts on his brothers rather than the reverse (Anima's heart, Tellus's death — see Cosmology).

    GapOpen — does the overwrite cost the River anything?

    Every other gift and act in this cosmology is paid for (Cosmology's "a price is always exacted"). Whether a River overwrite costs it something proportionate, or whether History genuinely outranks Agency here and the "equally strong" framing in River of Knowledge needs a caveat, is undecided.
