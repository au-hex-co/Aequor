Epilouge

Nexus lay on the ground, his whole body hurting, but the pain didn't feel bad to him. Finally rid of that corruption he felt peace.

As he looked at the ruined castle, he saw the gold tipped silver spear protruding from his chest. His head fell back on the rubble, his neck unable to support the head and Nexus looked into the sky which was no longer blocked by the ornate roof.

Blood filled his mouth, as he struggled to breathe. Yet he drew breath more easily than he had in the last two thousand years or so, he had lost count a long time ago. As he looked up he thought about his kingdom, his people. He remembered all the things he had done, a lone tear slowly slipped down his bloody cheek, landing on his bloody throne.

He softly traced the silver ornate spear that was keeping him down, this time however he saw her. She was laughing as they played in the meadow, watched by a massive tree. She was swinging a sword at a wooden dummy, being watched by a massive teal eye. She was planting a seed while humming the same tune as her mother. Finally she, in her late 30's was handing him an ornate silver spear with a gold point. The one that is in his chest. Exactly where it belonged.

Though he was mortal, the blood of a god was still flowing out of him leaving him with a couple more minutes to think about how he got here, when suddenly he saw movement to the left of him. With a massive effort he managed to slowly look to see his descendant, his children had long passed after all, Corvinus looked at his king. who lay on his broken throne with a massive pool of gold blood and stood shocked, rooted. Who would have expected such an outcome.

Managing to muster his strength Nexus smiled and slowly stammered through a mouthful of blood, "Little raven, It is my turn now. Swear to me child. Swear you will not be a king like me, swear you will lead the fae to a brighter future."

Corvinus who didn't know how to feel fell to his knees, his tears streaming down his face his pale white skin seemed to take on a silver hue as he swore, "I, In my name make this oath. I will lead the Fae to a brighter future, I will be a better..." as he tried to continue he couldn't he was unable to make that oath because he didn't think he would be a better king, how would he be a better king than FAE KING NEXUS? How would HE outdo the immortal king of honor?

"Swear it child." Nexus said

"I swear... I swear to be a better king than Nexus." Corvinus reluctantly said. The second the words left his mouth the air seemed to thicken. The blood that was pooling on the ground seemed to rise forming chains.

Entranced watching the golden blood rise Corvinus didn't move as the golden chains wrapped around his neck. The whole castle was filled with a lustrous silver light as Nexus said, "Little raven, we are all bound by duty, we all cannot escape fate. Yours is to lead your people. Now go, they need you, they need your protection, your reassurance."

Corvinus hesitated unwilling to leave his king... no father behind, but his oath pulled at him, he felt an urge to run away. The more he tried to fight it the more it fought back.

Reaching his breaking point Corvinus ran tears leaving a trail behind him as the gold blood solidified into a crown onto his head, the crown was made of gold with silver thorns, most point outwards but many pointing in. As the Fae looked up they collectively saw the marking. A new king was born. One who was crying, little Raven.

Nexus who lay alone in his throne room finally softly spoke, "It's just you and me. Now I see you again."

The Fae king died.
