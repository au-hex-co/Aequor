    Chapter 9: A God's Quarrel

    As the golden light settled, there was one final aftershock. A massive pulse of gold burst from the Aurora.

    Aurora then fell to the floor and started crying, the cry was ragged and sounded foreign.

    SLAM

    The door was pushed open by three elvish males who stood shocked watching Silvia hold the frail girl while she was slowly calming down.

    As the men stood speechless, Serena pushed past and showed a similar sign of shock at the wailing coming from Aurora.

    Lev who heard the commotion and saw the massive golden flash also ran full speed and barged through the people and saw...

    Silvia was on the floor, with the head of Aurora in her lap. As she looked at the elves they saw flecks of gold overwhelm her eyes, making them look more gold then silver.

    Finally breaking the silence one of the men yelled out, "The girl has been blessed by Anima, She is able to give life to the forsaken."

    This yell was met with a massive cheer as infectious as ever as it spread across the village.

    Serena walked up cautiously and asked, "How did you do it girl?"

    At first, Silvia thought Serena looked mad before she saw, Serena's eyes were filled with tears. And as Silvia watched she saw Serena approach Aurora and gently caress her.

    The second Serena touched the young girl, the girl flinched before leaning into the hand and nuzzling it. "What's her name?" Serena asked.

    "What do you mean?" Silvia stammered still shaken.

    "Lord Anima always named those he breathed life in.. So what do you name her?"

    Silvia finally spoke, "Aurora."

    Following Serena's lead the elves took Aurora and took her to get food and get cleaned up, leaving only Lev and Silvia behind.

    "So that's why" Lev said. "That's why you care for the forsaken, because you can help them."

    Though he said it neutrally he felt conflicted

    Silvia was quiet before finally saying, "I saw Anima."

    Lev instantly was dumbfounded before rushing Silvia and whispering, "Tell me more."

    Though Silvia was stunned by the events before her she retold from seeing the city gates and finally meeting the chained god.

    Upon hearing Anima's condition Lev was horrified.
